# Dorm Mart marketplace

Dorm Mart lets students arrange local item exchanges. This glossary names the concepts that govern scheduled exchanges and electronic payments.

## Language

**Scheduled Purchase**:
An accepted arrangement between one buyer and one seller to exchange an item at a scheduled time.
_Avoid_: Appointment, order

**Electronic Payment**:
A real-money USD payment that a buyer sends directly to a seller through an external payment provider for a Scheduled Purchase.
_Avoid_: Payment system, platform-held payment

**Payment Window**:
The 30-minute period that starts at the Scheduled Purchase time. An Electronic Payment must succeed before this period ends.
_Avoid_: Active period, checkout window
